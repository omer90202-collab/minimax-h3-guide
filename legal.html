02 · Privacy policy

This site collects nothing about you. No analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no advertising network, no contact form, no accounts.

The one thing stored on your device

When you pick a language, that choice is saved in your browser's localStorage under the key h3lang. It never leaves your device.

06 · Accessibility statement

This site was built to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the standard IS 5568 is based on.

This is a good-faith statement by the site's author, not a formal declaration of conformance. No audit by a certified accessibility professional has been carried out.

The accessibility menu

Every page carries an accessibility button in the bottom corner, with larger text, high contrast, highlighted links, a readable font, a large cursor and a switch that stops every animation. Choices are saved on your device only.

What is in place

  • Full keyboard navigation, visible focus outlines, and a skip-to-content link.
  • Text contrast meets or exceeds 4.5:1, with an automatic higher-contrast palette.
  • Animations stop entirely under reduce-motion, and no content depends on animation to be readable.
  • Videos are muted by default with standard controls and descriptive labels; decorative imagery is hidden from screen readers.
  • Fully responsive with no horizontal scrolling; text scales with browser zoom.
  • Both languages are marked up correctly, with document language and reading direction switching together.

Known limitations

The demo videos arrive from MiniMax without captions and we cannot caption them accurately; their content is described in the surrounding text. Scroll scenes are decorative and every fact they carry also appears as plain text.
